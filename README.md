# Privacy Policy for all blubsbuar Studios Applications

At blubsbuar Studios, we value your privacy. This privacy policy applies to all of our applications, including Custom Clicker and Rebinder.

We do not collect any personal data or track any user activity in our apps. Our apps are designed to function without the need to collect or store any user information on our side.

## Data stored on your device

Some apps save settings on your device so they can work as expected. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

**Rebinder** stores your key remappings in the Windows registry (`HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Keyboard Layout`). Remappings that are switched off and your preferred keyboard layout are stored in your local app data folder. To detect a key, Rebinder briefly reads keyboard input, only after you select "Press a key", and stops after the first key you press. What you type is never recorded or saved.
